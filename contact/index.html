COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a response?

All enquiries receive a response within 48 business hours. For urgent requirements, please state so in your message.

Can I request samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We strongly encourage sampling for new buyers. Sample charges apply and are credited against your first bulk order.

What are your payment terms?

Standard terms are 30% advance, 50% during production, 20% balance before shipment. Letter of Credit is also accepted.

Do you offer private label manufacturing?

Yes. We produce under your brand with custom labels, packaging, and bespoke design development available.

Which countries do you ship to?

We currently serve buyers in the USA, UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, Africa, the Caribbean, and more. We ship to most international destinations.
