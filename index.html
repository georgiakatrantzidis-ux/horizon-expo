Meet Our Founders

A family-led export house built on the foundation of Agra's world-renowned manufacturing capabilities.

Bharati Dhanwani founded Horizon Exports on a single conviction: that Agra's leather heritage deserved a global platform. She built the footwear division from scratch, developing relationships with international buyers across the US, UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, Africa, and the Caribbean.

As the business grew, buyers began asking for more. "Can you support apparel too?" It was a natural question, because Agra has always been known not only for leather craftsmanship, but also for skilled garment production and export-ready manufacturing.

Sagar and Siddharth Dhanwani joined as Co-Founders and expanded Horizon Exports into a broader sourcing and manufacturing house, covering footwear, men's and women's apparel, uniforms, accessories, and precious metal commodities. Each category followed buyer demand. That is why Horizon Exports feels coherent: everything we do, we do because our buyers needed it.

OUR HERITAGE

"Agra has been the home of master leather artisans and skilled tailors for centuries. When our buyers asked us to do both, we already had the city's heritage behind us. Horizon Exports doesn't do suits and shoes by coincidence. We do both because Agra does both."

— BHARATI DHANWANI, CEO

For centuries, Agra has been celebrated for two things: its master leather artisans and its skilled garment makers. It is not a coincidence that we do both, it is the natural result of being rooted in this city. When you source from Horizon Exports, you are not working with a generalist. You are working with a manufacturer that is deeply embedded in the two crafts that define our region, and that gives you factory-level expertise across both, from a single, accountable partner.
