About Horizon Exports

A Decade of Manufacturing Excellence and Global Trade

OUR STORY

Rooted in Agra's Heritage

Horizon Exports was established over a decade ago in Agra, Uttar Pradesh — a city with centuries of heritage in leather craftsmanship and garment making. What began as a focused export operation has grown into a multi-product manufacturing and merchant export business, trusted by buyers across fifteen countries.


Under the leadership of Bharati Dhanwani, CEO & Owner, Horizon Exports has built its reputation on three principles: uncompromising quality, transparent business practices, and genuine partnership with every buyer we work with.


We are not simply a supplier. We are a manufacturing partner that understands the demands of international trade — from product development and sampling through to bulk production, quality inspection, documentation, and delivery.

OUR DUAL ADVANTAGE

Manufacturer + Merchant Exporter
A Rare Combination

Most exporters are one or the other. Horizon Exports is both.

As a Direct Manufacturer

We control the full production process for our suiting and leather footwear lines — allowing us to offer factory-direct pricing, consistent quality, and flexible customisation without the delays or markups of intermediary trading.

As a Merchant Exporter

We leverage a carefully curated network of verified Indian manufacturers to source virtually any product category on behalf of our clients — making us a true one-stop sourcing partner for international buyers with diverse requirements.
