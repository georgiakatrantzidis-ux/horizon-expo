OUR STORY

Rooted in Agra's Heritage

Bharati Dhanwani founded Horizon Exports on a single conviction: that Agra's leather heritage deserved a global platform. She built the footwear division from scratch, developing relationships with international buyers across the US, UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, Africa, and the Caribbean.

As the business grew, buyers began asking for more. "Can you support apparel too?" It was a natural question, because Agra has always been known not only for leather craftsmanship, but also for skilled garment production and export-ready manufacturing.

Sagar and Siddharth Dhanwani joined as Co-Founders and expanded Horizon Exports into a broader sourcing and manufacturing house, covering footwear, men's and women's apparel, uniforms, accessories, and precious metal commodities. Each category followed buyer demand. That is why Horizon Exports feels coherent: everything we do, we do because our buyers needed it.

The question isn't why we make both suits and shoes. The question is, why would you source them separately? Agra has been the home of both crafts for centuries. Horizon Exports brings them together for one reason: to give you a single accountable partner for everything formal.

OUR DUAL ADVANTAGE

Manufacturer + Merchant Exporter
A Rare Combination

Most exporters are one or the other. Horizon Exports is both.

As a Direct Manufacturer

We control the full production process for our suiting and leather footwear lines, allowing us to offer factory-direct pricing, consistent quality, and flexible customisation without the delays or markups of intermediary trading.

As a Merchant Exporter

We leverage a carefully curated network of verified Indian manufacturers to source virtually any product category on behalf of our clients, making us a true one-stop sourcing partner for international buyers with diverse requirements.
